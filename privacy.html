Privacy Policy

How we protect and manage your personal data.

  • Data Collection: We only collect necessary information (name, email, shipping address) required to fulfill your orders and provide customer support.
  • Payment Security: All payment transactions are encrypted and processed through secure third-party gateways; we do not store your credit card details.
  • No Third-Party Selling: We value your privacy and will never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to outside companies.
  • Cookies: Our website uses cookies to remember your cart items and improve your browsing experience.
  • Your Rights: You have the right to request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account information at any time by contacting support.
