STITCH CULTURE - STORE POLICIES AND TERMS OF SERVICE

1. RETURNS AND REFUNDS POLICY
- We offer a 30-day return window from the date of delivery for all Stitch Culture products.
- Items must be unworn, unwashed, and retain all original tags and packaging. 
- Leather goods (jackets, boots) must not show any crease marks or scuffs to be eligible for a return.
- Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we inspect the returned item.
- Clearance items and customized products are strictly non-refundable.

2. SHIPPING AND DELIVERY POLICY
- Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days.
- Standard shipping is free on all orders over ₹5,000. For orders under ₹5,000, a flat shipping fee of ₹150 applies.
- Expedited shipping (1-2 business days) is available for an additional ₹300.
- Once an order is dispatched, customers will receive a tracking link via email and SMS.

3. WARRANTY AND PRODUCT GUARANTEE
- Premium Watches: All Stitch Culture watches come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering internal movement defects.
- Leather Jackets & Boots: We offer a 6-month warranty against hardware failures (e.g., broken zippers, faulty snaps).
- The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, water damage, or accidental scratches.

4. PRIVACY AND DATA PROTECTION
- We collect personal data (name, email, address) solely to process orders and provide customer support.
- We use secure encryption for all payment transactions. We do not store credit card or UPI details on our servers.
- We strictly protect user privacy and never sell or share customer data with third-party advertisers.
